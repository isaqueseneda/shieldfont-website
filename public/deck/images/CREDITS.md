# Deck image credits

These images support the presentation **How to poison unauthorized AI training
with a typeface**, by S&A + Playtype.

## Made by S&A and Playtype

- `title-card.webp`: the title card from the opening film
  (`/videos/shieldfont-tutorial.mp4`), the frame at 41.08 seconds, 1920 × 1080.
  Used for the Presenting ShieldFont slide.
- `encoder.webp`: a screenshot of the site's own encoder page, shieldfont.org/encoder.
- `../opentype/*.svg`: the seven pages of "A quick note on OpenType", the type
  designer's own slides. They are the vectors of `ShieldFontPres.pdf`, converted
  with `scripts/deck-opentype-svg.py`; the PDF is kept in
  `archive/deck/2026-10-02-opentype-note/`.
