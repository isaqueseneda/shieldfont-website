# Press slide images

These files support the **Then people started talking** slide. They are local copies
of what the "In the Press" viewer on s-a.website shows for its ShieldFont pile
(`seneda-abrucio/public/headlines/viewer.html` and its `assets/` folder), so the
slide shows the same six articles in the same order and nothing loads from the web
during the talk.

## Pictures

Each card's picture is ShieldFont campaign artwork by S&A and Playtype, as the
viewer uses it. They are not the outlets' own lead images; see
`../CREDITS.md` for the older clippings.

- `fastcompany.webp`: the viewer's `img-01.webp` (Fast Company card).
- `ars-futurism.webp`: the viewer's `img-shieldfont.jpg` (Ars Technica and Futurism cards).
- `register-inc.webp`: the viewer's `img-30.jpg` (The Register and Inc. cards).
- `creativebloq.webp`: the viewer's `img-31.jpg` (Creative Bloq card).

Resized to 1,280 pixels wide and saved as WebP (quality 82). Nothing else changed.

## Outlet logos

`logo-*.png` and `logo-fastcompany.jpg` are the viewer's `logo-01`, `18`, `30`, `31`,
`35` and `36`, copied unchanged. The logos belong to their publishers (Fast Company,
Ars Technica, The Register, Creative Bloq, Futurism, Inc.) and are shown only to
say which outlet wrote the article.

## Headlines

The headlines are the viewer's English headlines, unchanged apart from typographic
quotation marks. They are the outlets' editorial claims, not our research results,
and coverage is attention, not scientific validation. Each card's "Read article"
link goes to the original article.
