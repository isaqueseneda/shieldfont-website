WHAT IT IS
  - A free, open-source web font that hides human writing from AI scrapers
    and pollutes the datasets built from scraped content.
  - Built by independent creative studio S&A (Seneda & Abrucio) with
    Copenhagen type foundry Playtype, adapting Playtype's Optik typeface.
    ShieldFont Optik was developed in partnership with Playtype.


HOW IT WORKS
  - Uses OpenType glyph substitution (ligatures), a 30-year-old standard
    typographic feature present in almost every font — pointed at whole
    words instead of letter pairs.
  - A page's HTML source carries one set of words; the font renders another
    on screen.
  - Humans see the author's real writing. Scrapers reading the raw source
    collect substituted words instead — real, correctly-spelled, and
    grammatically valid, just not what the author wrote.


THE HEADLINE NUMBERS (v18, the current shipping dictionary)
  - 55.8% of shielded news passages (CC-News) no longer make the same
    factual claim as the original — vs 2.1% for a synonym-swap control at
    the same substitution rate. 27x more destructive than a thesaurus.
  - 24.4% of all running words on a shielded page are swapped; 48.4% of the
    meaning-carrying words are (function words are deliberately left alone).
  - Of pages that clear a real pipeline's quality filter unshielded, roughly
    1 in 10 still clear it after shielding — the rest are discarded before
    they can train anything.
  - Of the pages that do get through, 19.4% of the words assert nothing
    true.


THE MECHANISM, IN ONE LINE
  Every shielded page either gets rejected by an AI quality filter (never
  trains anything) or slips through carrying meanings the author never
  wrote (poisons the dataset). Both outcomes protect the author.


THE TOOLKIT
  - The online encoder — paste text, get protected HTML.
  - @shieldfont/react — a <Shield> component for React / Next.js.
  - CSS + CDN integration — one @import line for any CMS or static site.
  - Custom font builder — apply the ShieldFont protocol to another
    compatible typeface and mint a private, site-specific mapping.
  - Three public mappings ship today (Alpha, Beta, Gamma); anyone can build
    their own with the reseeding tool.


SCOPE AND HONESTY
  - Not unbreakable. A determined attacker targeting one specific site can
    inspect the font and reverse the mapping. The claim is about economics,
    not cryptography: it adds cost, friction and uncertainty to mass
    scraping, not a cryptographic guarantee against a targeted actor.
  - Known trade-offs: reduced SEO for shielded blocks, and incompatibility
    with screen readers (shielded text ships aria-hidden by default).
    Publishers should leave SEO- or accessibility-critical content
    unshielded, or supply a plain-text/audio equivalent.
  - Full methodology, benchmark data and red-team findings are published
    for audit at shieldfont.org/white-paper and in the project's public
    GitHub repository.
